Organ-Specific Diagnostic Testing

Select a specialized pathology module below to view exact test inclusions and baseline clinic pricing parameters.

🩺 Pulmonary & Lungs

₹1,499

Evaluates respiratory system volumes, airway obstructions, lung field clarity, and gas exchange efficiencies.

  • Chest X-Ray (PA View): Detects fluid accumulation, infections, or enlargement.
  • Spirometry / PFT: Assesses total air inhalation and exhalation lung capacity.
  • HRCT Thorax Preview: Advanced screening for chronic obstructive pulmonary issues.

❤️ Cardiology & Heart

₹2,499

Analyzes electrical path mapping, mechanical valve functions, arterial structural stress, and lipid counts.

  • 12-Lead Electrocardiogram (ECG): Captures immediate cardiovascular rhythmic stability.
  • 2D Echocardiography: Ultrasound visual mapping of functional ejection fraction rate.
  • Extended Lipid Profile: Measures breakdown of HDL, LDL, and total cholesterol.

🧼 Renal & Kidney

₹1,199

Monitors organic cellular filtration rates, toxin clearance speeds, and blood electrolyte balances.

  • Serum Creatinine & Urea: Direct metabolic waste clearance performance metrics.
  • Uric Acid Levels: Evaluates processing of complex purine crystalline byproducts.
  • Routine Urinalysis: Screens for leakage patterns of microalbumin or protein cells.

🧪 Hepatic & Liver

₹1,299

Measures enzyme health, structural cellular damage, toxic breakdown limits, and vital protein manufacturing.

  • SGOT & SGPT Bilirubin: Screens for active cellular inflammation or liver damage.
  • Alkaline Phosphatase (ALP): Checks structural bile duct passage flow rates.
  • Albumin / Globulin Ratio: Measures fluid balance and internal protein levels.

🩸 Diabetes & Thyroid

₹999

Tracks core metabolic health, hormonal balances, and long-term glucose stabilization curves.

  • HbA1c Glycated Hemoglobin: Evaluates three-month average tracking of glucose.
  • Thyroid Profile (T3, T4, TSH): Regulates metabolic system processing speed.
  • Fasting Blood Sugar (FBS): Immediate baseline index for blood glucose.

🦴 Orthopedic & Bone

₹1,799

Assesses critical architectural structural density parameters, mineral stocks, and joint wear biomarkers.

  • Vitamin D3 (25-Hydroxy): Essential element marker for calcium absorption stability.
  • Serum Calcium & Phosphorus: Raw material assessment for mineralized skeletal mass.
  • Rheumatoid Factor (RF): Primary immune indicator for complex joint inflammation.

🧠 Neurology & Brain

₹2,999

Evaluates electrical brain activity, structural integrity, and nerve conduction for comprehensive neurological assessment.

  • MRI Brain (Plain): High-resolution imaging for tumors, stroke, or structural anomalies.
  • EEG (Electroencephalogram): Measures brain wave patterns to detect epilepsy or sleep disorders.
  • Nerve Conduction Velocity (NCV): Assesses peripheral nerve damage and muscle response times.

🍽️ Gastrointestinal & Digestive

₹1,899

Comprehensive evaluation of digestive tract function, enzyme levels, and gut microbiome health.

  • Upper GI Endoscopy: Visual inspection of esophagus, stomach, and duodenum.
  • H. pylori Antigen Test: Detects bacterial infection causing ulcers and gastritis.
  • Liver Function Test (LFT) Extended: Includes GGT and total protein for deeper hepatic insight.
