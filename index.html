Our Legacy

Established in 1996 in Hyderabad by the late Sultan Salahuddin Owaisi, Owaisi Hospital was built to provide affordable medical care and clinical training for underserved communities.

Managed by the Dar-us-Salam Educational Trust, it grew into a major multi-specialty teaching hospital linked with the Deccan College of Medical Sciences.

Today it stands as a symbol of accessible healthcare and medical education in the old city.

Sultan Salahuddin Owaisi

Founder of the hospital (1996). A philanthropist who envisioned low-cost healthcare and medical training in Hyderabad.

Barrister Asaduddin Owaisi

Chairman of the Owaisi Group of Hospitals and Deccan Educational Trust. MP for Hyderabad and President of AIMIM.

Akbaruddin Owaisi

Oversees institutional expansions and conducts medical audits, maintaining a close administrative connection.

About Owaisi Hospital

A major multi-specialty teaching hospital at Kanchan Bagh, Santosh Nagar, Hyderabad. Established in 1996, it provides affordable 24/7 emergency, diagnostic, and advanced clinical care.

Infrastructure & Operations

Located at DMRL Cross Road, Santosh Nagar, this seven-storied hospital is divided into four specialised wings spanning 700,000 sq. ft.

Optimal segregation of healthcare delivery ensures efficient patient flow and advanced medical care.

Academic & Social Welfare

Primary teaching hospital for Deccan College of Medical Sciences with 1,000 beds, bridging academic medicine and affordable care.

Provides subsidised or free services including cataract surgeries and circumcisions, plus a Centre for Liver Diseases.
