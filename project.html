The Owaisi Hospital & Research Centre is a prominent healthcare landmark in Hyderabad, Telangana, established in 1996 under the visionary leadership of Janab Sultan Salahuddin Owaisi Sahab and the Darussalam Educational Trust. Strategically located at the DMRL Cross Road in Santosh Nagar, Kanchan Bagh, this premier institution stands as a massive seven-storied infrastructure meticulously structured into four specialized wings to streamline its expansive internal operations. Spanning a monumental area of 700,000 square feet, the architectural design ensures optimal segregation of healthcare delivery, positioning the administration blocks and daycare services in the North wing while dedicating the East wing strictly to private consultation rooms and superior clinician suites. The South wing operates entirely as a high-capacity inpatient ward, seamlessly connecting with the West wing, which houses heavily equipped critical care units, a dedicated pediatric facility, and an advanced 24-bed operation theatre complex built to handle multi-specialty surgical emergencies around the clock.The daily logistics and patient transitions are run smoothly by a massive workforce of over 1,000 to 5,000 personnel—including 209 academic staff, skilled resident medical officers, paramedical teams, and highly trained nursing staff who consistently maintain clean, hygienic facilities despite massive daily out-patient footfalls. To complement this vast internal medical ecosystem, external transit has also been modernized via infrastructure like Hyderabad's longest steel flyover at the Owaisi Hospital Junction, drastically reducing emergency response times and providing immediate regional accessibility to patients traveling from all over Telangana

Serving as the primary teaching hospital for the affiliated Deccan College of Medical Sciences, the institution features an impressive 1,000-bed capacity, bridging the gap between rigorous academic medicine and affordable healthcare for urban communities. Internally, the hospital operates a robust clinical setup spanning 12 distinct medical specializations, including comprehensive departments for Cardiology, General Surgery, Gastroenterology, Orthopedics, Urology, and Neonatology, alongside an active General Medicine division that treats everything from complex geriatric cases to acute pediatric illnesses. The hospital's foundational mission centers tightly on providing highly subsidised or completely free medical services to underprivileged families, famously delivering free cataract surgeries, free circumcisions, and free general ward stays including three daily meals. Furthermore, the obstetrics department significantly lowers economic barriers by offering lower segment cesarean section (LSCS) operations at highly competitive, subsidized rates. This deep-rooted commitment to social welfare is amplified by the hospital's active, long-term empanelment with the Telangana state government’s Aarogyasri Scheme since 2007, facilitating completely cashless, high-end surgeries and intensive treatments for the Below Poverty Line (BPL) population across the old city of Hyderabad. Beyond basic patient welfare, the campus incorporates cutting-edge medical science through its dedicated research wing, the Centre for Liver Diseases, which draws major national funding to spearhead complex experiments in liver cell transplantation, xenotransplantation, gene therapy.
